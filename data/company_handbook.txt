
COMPANY HANDBOOK

Document 1: Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to three days per week
with manager approval. Employees must use the company VPN
when accessing internal systems remotely.

Document 2: Password Policy
Employees must use passwords of at least 14 characters.
Multi-factor authentication is required for internal systems.
Passwords must never be shared with colleagues.

Document 3: Security Incident Reporting
Employees must report suspected phishing emails to the
security team immediately. Security incidents should be
reported through the internal security portal.

Document 4: Data Classification
Public data may be shared externally.
Internal data is restricted to company employees.
Confidential data requires explicit authorization.
Restricted documents must only be accessed by approved users.
